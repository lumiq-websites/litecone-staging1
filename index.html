The problemThe unfinished workflow

Financial-services decisions are split
across steps that never meet.

A case is read, checked, assessed, decided, written up and filed. Software handles some steps; people handle the rest. Each piece completes its task, but no one completes the case.

A solution finishes a step.
AI Co-workers complete the workflow.

A faster typewriter does not post a faster letter. The case moves between them by hand, and the decision at the end still waits on a person.

Co-DecisionThe distinction that matters

Assistants recommend.
Co-deciders decide.

An assistant drafts a recommendation and hands it back to a person, making that person the queue. A Co-decider carries the decision within the policy you set, escalates exceptions, and keeps accountability with you.

Faster people is not more capacity. More decisions is.
