Read the number honestly

01

This is about the work, not about anybody's software

There is no vendor named on this page and no licence being costed. We are not arguing that what you bought was a bad decision. We are asking how many hands a single case passes through before somebody can say yes or no.

02

The minutes are the ones worth arguing about

The opening figures are mid market observations, not measurements of your operation. Time one week of real cases, put those minutes in, and the answer stops being ours and starts being yours.

03

Some cases still need a person

The one pass side keeps two lines that never go to zero: the cases referred out, and the oversight minutes on the ones that complete. Set both to zero if you want the flattering version, but do not take that number into a board meeting.

04

Nobody staffs eight steps on day one

The realistic first workflow is one agent on one queue, doing three or four of these steps end to end. The full picture is here because it tells you where this goes, not what you sign in March.

The same arithmetic sits behind how this is priced, which is per decision rather than per seat.
