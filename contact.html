The briefingForty minutes

What happens in the forty minutes

  • Ten minutes on your queue. Volume, current handling cost, where it backs up and who it escalates to today.
  • Fifteen minutes on the mandate. What an agent could decide alone, what it must refer and what it may never touch.
  • Ten minutes on governance. The five brains, the audit trail and what your examiner will be shown.
  • Five minutes on what happens next. Either a shadow run on your data, or an honest no.
