The LUMIQ triadThree at once

The triad

Three capabilities, held at once. Most vendors in this category have one of them and subcontract the other two, which is the real reason so many of these programmes stop at pilot.

One

Data Co-workers

Agents that do the work underneath every decision. Ingestion, quality, reconciliation, lineage and the unglamorous plumbing that decides whether anything above it can be trusted. A decision agent standing on bad data is a liability with good manners.

Two

FSI Co-workers

The named agents that carry the decisions themselves. Underwriting, claims, intake and compliance, each with a boundary and an escalation rule. This is the part customers meet by name.

Three

Forward deployed engineers

People, not software. FSI engineers who sit inside your environment for the length of the deployment, and who have taken this into production inside leading banks and insurers before yours. They are the reason a pilot becomes a production system.

The data companies have the first. The AI startups have the second. The consultancies have the third. Very few have all three, and the gap between a demo and a live decision is exactly where the missing two show up.

Forward deployedPeople, in your environment

Why forward deployed matters here

A regulated institution cannot accept software that arrives with a login and a support address. The manual has exceptions nobody wrote down. The third page of the statement never parses cleanly. The escalation path exists in somebody's head.

Forward deployed engineers are how that gets resolved: in your environment, against your files, alongside your team, until the agent decides the way your best underwriter would.
