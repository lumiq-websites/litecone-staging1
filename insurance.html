The scopeThree buckets

Three parts of the business,
staffed the same way

Underwriting is the part everybody names first, and it is not the whole of it. The submission that arrives before it, the endorsement that follows it and the audit that comes after all run on the same reading work.

01

New business

From the submission landing to the risk being on the books.

  • SubmissionsWhat arrives is read, sorted and checked for completeness before anyone opens it.
  • Intake and onboardingDocuments read, data points pulled, the file assembled the way an underwriter would assemble it.
  • UnderwritingThe decision made against your manual, with the reasoning written at the moment it is made.
  • EndorsementsMid term changes handled as work in their own right rather than as an interruption to something else.
LEO, ERIC, RIO, AURA
02

Claims

From first notification to the payment leaving.

  • IntakeThe notification and everything attached to it read on arrival, with what is missing raised immediately.
  • AdjudicationAssessed against the policy wording and the plan rules, and settled, declined or referred.
  • AdmissibilityChecked against cover before the clinical or financial assessment begins, not after.
MARC, ARIS
03

Compliance

The evidence, assembled while the work happens.

  • Policy citationEvery decision carries the line of the manual it was made under.
  • Audit trailBuilt as the case runs, in the form an examiner asks for, rather than reconstructed later.
  • Oversight settingsWhat the workforce may decide on its own, held as policy and adjustable at any time.
