The definitionIn one paragraph

What Co-Decision means

The term is ours, so here is exactly what it commits us to.

Co-Decision means the agent makes the decision, inside boundaries you set, and accountability stays with you. You decide which cases it may take, on what policy, and where it has to stop. It decides those and acts on them. Everything outside comes back to you. You can widen the boundary, narrow it, or reverse any single decision, at any time.

You decideThe policy, the thresholds, the segments, and how much autonomy the agent gets. It is a dial, not a switch.
It decidesThe cases that sit inside those boundaries, with the reasoning and the citation written as it goes.
It refersEverything outside them, with the reason it stopped attached to the file.
You keepThe override on every decision, and the right to narrow or pause any agent without a change request.

ControlHow the system is governed

Three places you stay in control

Risk committees ask how this is governed before they ask anything else. The answer is that control sits in three places, all of them yours, and none of them requires you to open every case.

01

Guideline setting

Your policies, authored by your people and held as the operating rules. Thresholds, segments, what must always be referred. Versioned, and changeable in an afternoon rather than a release cycle.

02

Configurable oversight

How much the system runs on its own, set by you and adjustable at any time. Widen it where the evidence supports it, narrow it or pause an agent entirely without raising a change request.

03

Case execution

Every case cites the policy line it was decided under, stays grounded in your data and your manual, and leaves an auditable log in the form an examiner asks for.
