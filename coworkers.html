The workforceNine Co-workers

Meet the AI Co-workers.

Each one holds a role in a financial institution, the way a person in that seat would. Underneath each is a bench of agents doing the individual parts of a case, and the Co-worker is accountable for the whole of it.

How to read thisCo-workers and agents

A Co-worker is a role.
An agent is a task.

The Co-workerNamed, with a job title an insurer or a bank would recognise. It holds the case from arrival to decision and carries the reasoning.
The agentOne task inside that case. Reading a document, cross-checking a figure, running a calculation, writing the memo.
ScrutinyThe bench that runs first on every case, on every Co-worker. Intake, Vision and Veritas.
AccountabilityNot on either of them. It stays with you, and the boundary you set decides what the Co-worker may take.
